About Me

I am a Master's student in Computer Technology at Shanghai University of Electric Power. My research primarily focuses on Large Language Models (LLMs), with special emphasis on model editing, LLM security (including backdoor and jailbreak attacks), and trustworthy artificial intelligence. I am passionate about building secure, reliable, and interpretable machine learning systems.

Analyzing vulnerabilities, backdoor attacks, jailbreak defenses, and copyright protection in open-source AI models.

Trustworthy AI

About Me

I am a master's student in Computer Technology at Shanghai University of Electric Power. My research focuses on large language models, with particular interests in model editing, LLM security, backdoor and jailbreak attacks, and trustworthy artificial intelligence.

Research Interests

Large Language Models
Security and reliability of modern large language models.
Model Editing
Efficient modification and analysis of model knowledge and behavior.
LLM Security
Jailbreak attacks, backdoors, and security risks in open-weight language models.
Trustworthy AI
Robustness, attribution, watermarking, and trustworthy deployment of AI systems.
